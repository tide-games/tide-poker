Provably fair — how two tides deal one hand

In Tide mode your stake and a random mark name a testnet4 block that does not exist yet. When it is mined (plus one confirmation — reorgs are real), your five cards are the top of a full-deck shuffle seeded by sha256(blockA | mark). You choose your holds, and the discards are replaced from a second shuffle — of the 47 cards the deal left — seeded by the next block: sha256(blockB | mark). Your decision sits between two randomnesses; the replacement cards exist for nobody when you choose. Both stages recompute from public chain data — verify ✓ in the log does it in your browser, and reports honestly if the chain reorged under a settled hand.

The paytable is classic 8/5 Jacks-or-Better — about a 2.7% house edge with perfect play, and your skill at choosing holds genuinely matters. The maths is open in poker.js, pure and testable.

Practice mode is seeded locally and plays at once — pure fun, not provable.
